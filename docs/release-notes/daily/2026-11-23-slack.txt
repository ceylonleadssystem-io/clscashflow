:rocket: *Ceylonry POS: new features and fixes (2026-11-23)*

*1. Live support session (#179)*
Support can help a client live with nothing to install. The client reads out a one-time code, support joins from the admin portal and guides them on their own screen.
• 6-digit code, works once, expires in 10 minutes
• Typed text, PINs and passwords are hidden from support
• Support can draw on the client's screen (Pointer, Highlighter, Pen, Arrow, Spotlight, Note) and ask to open a POS screen
• Support can click, type and scroll only if the client allows it. Sign-in, PIN, payment and delete actions always stay with the client
• Reconnects by itself for up to 2 minutes. The connection line shows Direct or Through the relay, and Slow above 500 ms

*Client: start a session*
1. Open *Settings > Plan & Support*
2. In *Live support*, click *Get support code*
3. Read the 6 digits to Ceylonry Support
4. Click *End session* at any time to stop

*Client: when support asks*
1. "Support asks to open ...": *Open it* or *Not now*
2. "Support asks to click and type ...": *Allow* or *Not now*
3. Click *Pause control* to take control back

*Support: join and guide*
1. Sign in to the admin portal, open *Live support*
2. Enter the 6-digit code and click *Join*
3. Pick a tool and colour and draw; *Clear* removes drawings
4. *Take the client to...* then *Send* opens a POS screen for them
5. *Ask to control*, then use *Control*; *Release* hands it back

*2. Support-only admin accounts*
A support account can use Live support and nothing else. Existing accounts stay full admins, no change needed. Create one with `node scripts/hash-admin-password.mjs` and answer "support" to "Account type" (run `node scripts/setup-admin-db.mjs` once first).

*3. Fixes and other*
• Settings pages (System Options, Users, Menu Admin, Device Manager) now scroll with mouse wheel and touch in full-screen mode
• Admin portal: "Open client's POS" and "Sign in as client (live)" removed, replaced by Live support
• Setup and relay guide: `docs/support-live-session.md`

:information_source: For networks that block direct connections a relay server must be set up first.
