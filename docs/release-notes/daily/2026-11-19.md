# Ceylonry POS: Release Notes, 2026-11-19

## Features

### 1. Online orders from Shopify
**What it does.** Orders placed in your Shopify store arrive in the POS as open orders, ready for the kitchen. Off until an admin turns on *Online orders (Shopify)* for the business.

**How it works.**
- Shopify sends each new or cancelled order to the POS through a signed webhook. New orders show up within about half a minute while the POS is open.
- Orders are saved at the location you choose, with the Shopify channel and a Shopify payment type that stays through payment.
- Shopify orders can be sent to the kitchen automatically, or wait until staff send them.
- If the store currency is not LKR, totals are converted with the rate you enter.
- Items are matched to your inventory by SKU. An item with no matching stock row shows a badge and its stock is not reduced.
- Optional e-mail alert for orders that arrive while no POS is open.

**Steps: connect the store**
1. Go to *Settings > System Options > Online Store*.
2. In Shopify admin open *Settings > Notifications > Webhooks* and create webhooks for *Order creation* and *Order cancellation* (JSON), using the *Webhook address* shown in the POS (use the copy button).
3. Copy the signing secret from Shopify.
4. In the POS enter *Store address*, *Location that prepares these orders*, *Store currency* (and *LKR for 1 unit* if not LKR), optionally an e-mail, and paste the *Webhook signing secret*.
5. Press *Connect*.
6. Tick *Send each new online order to the kitchen as soon as it arrives* if you want automatic sending.

**Steps: work the orders**
1. Click the *Online orders* cart button in the top bar (a badge shows new orders).
2. The page shows *Online Orders*, *New*, *Sent to Kitchen* and *Value*. Open an order to prepare it.

### 2. Shopify customers in the CRM
**What it does.** Shopify buyers are added to the CRM automatically, matched by phone or e-mail so repeat buyers are not duplicated.

**Steps**
1. Open *CRM*.
2. Look for the *Shopify* badge, the online order count and spend, and the *Delivery address* on a customer.
3. To list them, use the filter and choose *Online (Shopify) customers* (shown once such customers exist).

### 3. Online Orders report
**What it does.** Orders, Shopify number, customer, items, totals and share of sales. Online orders are no longer counted in the In-Store Channels report.

**Steps:** open *Reports > Sales > Online Orders* (shown only where online orders are on).

### 4. Items with short ingredients are blocked when selected
**What it does.** At the sales screen, an item whose ingredients cannot cover one more unit (counting what is already on the order) is greyed out and shows "Not enough <ingredient>". Adding or raising the quantity shows which ingredient is short, instead of failing at payment.

### 5. Manager override for low stock
**What it does.** A manager's PIN lets a short-stock item go on the order anyway.

**Steps**
1. Tap the short item, or raise its quantity.
2. In *Manager approval* enter the *Manager or owner PIN*.
3. Press *Approve and add*. The line passes the payment check and the approval is logged.
Roles with the permission *Sell low-stock items without a manager* skip the PIN.

### 6. Ceylo AI: pick the model from a list, with a key guide
**What it does.** On the Ceylo AI page, the model is now chosen from a list loaded from your AI provider instead of typed in. A guide on the right explains how to get an API key for Claude (Anthropic), ChatGPT (OpenAI) or Gemini (Google).

**How it works.** Once you paste a key (or if the location already has a saved key), the POS asks the provider which models the account can use. If the list cannot load, you can type the model name instead. Usage is billed by the provider. Without a key, Ceylo uses Ceylonry's service when the plan includes it.

**Steps**
1. Go to *Settings > System Options > Ceylo AI*.
2. Pick the *Location*, then the *AI provider*.
3. Follow *How to get a ... API key* on the right, then paste the key into *API key*.
4. Choose a *Model* from the list.
5. Press *Save & test key*. Use *Remove key* to go back to Ceylonry's AI.

### 7. QR menu: admin switch
**What it does.** The QR menu is now off by default and is turned on per business by an admin (admin portal, feature group *Online orders & QR menu*, switch *QR menu*). The *QR Menu* settings page shows only where it is on. Turning the switch off stops the printed codes from working; turning it on again restores the same links.

**Steps (once on)**
1. Go to *Settings > System Options > QR Menu*.
2. With several locations, pick one under *Location*.
3. Press *Create QR menu* (later *Turn QR menu back on*).
4. Press *Download QR code* and print it. Use *Send latest menu now* to push menu changes at once.

## Fixes
- Menu Admin no longer crashes when an item with no saved locations has its location changed (#161).
- The *Syncing* button was removed from the top bar. A red sync indicator now shows only when the device is offline or the last sync failed (tap to retry).
