## Features

### QR menu (digital menu for restaurants and cafés)
**What it does.** Gives each location one fixed QR code. Customers scan it with their phone camera and see your menu: categories, items, descriptions, prices and photos.

**How it works.**
- The code never changes. When you edit items, prices or categories, the menu behind it updates within a few seconds while the POS is open, so a printed code stays valid.
- With several locations, each has its own code showing only the items sold there. "All locations" shows every item.
- Photos stored only on one device are not shown; only https image links appear. Stock and cost are never shown.

**Steps**
1. Go to **Settings > System Options > QR Menu**.
2. If you have more than one location, pick it under **Location**.
3. Press **Create QR menu** (later: **Turn QR menu back on**).
4. Press **Download QR code** and print it for tables, the counter or the door.
5. Use **Copy link** or **Open menu** to check the page, **Send latest menu now** to push changes at once, or **Turn off** to stop the code working.

### New landing page
**What it does.** The Ceylonry POS website was replaced with a new page: before/after story, 20 industries, a plan finder, 4-step onboarding, FAQ and a contact section. Scroll animations are layered on top.

### Plan finder: devices per location
**What it does.** The plan finder now asks how many devices each location needs and prices the plan to match. Devices go up in steps of 5, from 5 to 50.

**How it works.**
- Each Business plan covers up to 5 devices.
- Every extra 5 devices adds another Business plan (10 devices = 2 plans, 15 = 3, and so on).
- From 30 devices per location the finder switches to Pro (unlimited devices).
- Monthly price, annual price and annual saving are multiplied by the number of plans and locations.

**Steps**
1. Open the website and go to **Plans & pricing**.
2. Under **How many devices per location?**, press **+** or **−** (starts at 5).
3. Under **How many locations?**, press **+** or **−**.
4. Read the recommended plan, for example "Business × 2", and its price. The line below explains why.
