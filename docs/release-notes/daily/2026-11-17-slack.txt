*Plan finder: devices per location*
Devices go up in steps of 5 (5 to 50). Each Business plan covers up to 5 devices, and every extra 5 devices adds another Business plan. From 30 devices per location it recommends Pro (unlimited devices). Prices scale with plans and locations.
1. Go to *Plans & pricing* on the website
2. Under *How many devices per location?* press + or − (starts at 5)
3. Under *How many locations?* press + or −
4. Read the recommended plan (e.g. "Business × 2"), its price and the reason below it
