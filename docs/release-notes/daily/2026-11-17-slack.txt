:rocket: *Ceylonry POS release notes: 2026-11-17*

*Features*
• *QR menu (issue #158):* one fixed QR code per location opens your menu on a customer's phone (categories, items, descriptions, prices, photos). The code never changes; edits to items, prices or categories show up on the same code within a few seconds while the POS is open. Each location's code shows only items sold there.
1. Go to *Settings > System Options > QR Menu*.
2. Pick the *Location* (if you have several).
3. Press *Create QR menu*.
4. Press *Download QR code* and print it.
5. Use *Copy link*, *Open menu*, *Send latest menu now* or *Turn off* as needed.
• *New website:* before/after story, 20 industries, plan finder, 4-step onboarding, FAQ and contact, with scroll animations and a visual refresh.
• *Plan finder:* choose devices per location (steps of 5, 5 to 50) and locations. Each Business plan covers up to 5 devices; every extra 5 adds a plan; Pro from 30 devices. Find it under *Plans & pricing* on the website.
