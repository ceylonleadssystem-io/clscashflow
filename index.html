Do I need to buy new hardware?+

No. Start on the phone, tablet, iPad, laptop or desktop you already own. Our hardware is optional.

Can I use it on an iPad or touchscreen?+

Yes. Open Ceylonry POS in Safari and tap "Add to Home Screen". It works like an app.

What happens if the internet goes down?+

Offline mode keeps you selling. Everything syncs when you are back online.

How long does setup take?+

Standard setup takes 1–3 working days. Menu or product data usually takes 3–4 hours once we have complete information.

Can I create my own categories?+

Yes. Set up categories, items and prices the way your business works, or let our team do it from your files.

Is hardware included in the subscription?+

No. Your subscription covers the software. Hardware is a separate, optional purchase.

Is there a free trial?+

Yes, 7 days free. No card needed.
