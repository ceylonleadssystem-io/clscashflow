Questions

Before you start.

Can I use CeylonryPOS on an iPad or touchscreen?

Yes. Checkout is touch-ready and can be installed from Safari using Add to Home Screen for a dedicated full-screen register experience.

What happens if the internet disconnects?

The POS keeps changes on the device while offline and automatically synchronises them when the connection returns.

Can I use my own categories and products?

Yes. Business presets provide a starting point, while categories, products, services, modifiers, prices and stock remain customisable.

Does the plan include hardware?

The subscription covers the POS software. Compatible hardware and future bundles will be listed separately in the hardware shop section.
